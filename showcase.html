Generating Feedback Poetry

Education, Professional Development

The Idea

Exploring the use of AI feedback poetry to engage students with the feedback they receive and give in new ways. Evidence suggests that students often don't engage with the feedback they receive. Feedback that has been put together by educators with care.

What I Aim to Achieve

We know that poetry and storytelling more generally can have connecting powers. Could AI poetry created with human input help educators communicate the key feedback message and also potentially help students engage in peer-to-peer feedback using feedback poetry? After all, feedback works well when it is dialogic. Could it also reduce the amount and time educators spend on providing feedback?

Where the Inspiration Comes From

I have used alternative feedback approaches in the past, including audio feedback, which can bring the message alive and can create a real connection between the giver and the receiver. While I was exploring ChatGPT, the idea of feedback poetry emerged at a time I was marking students' work as a way to perhaps engage students in a different way with the feedback they receive and potentially reduce feedback load and time spent on it.
